# Hyfens Trademarks

> Draft — subject to maintainer and legal review.

This is a conservative draft policy for the names and visual identifiers used
by the Hyfens project. It is not legal advice. It does not amend the
[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE), grant a source-code license, or make a finding
that any mark is registered.

## Names and identifiers

The name “Hyfens,” the `hyfens` command name, the Hyfens logo, and other names
or artwork presented as official Hyfens identifiers may distinguish the
project and its official materials. The presence of a name or asset in this
repository does not, by itself, grant permission to use it as a trademark.

For this draft, use the following factual attribution:

> Hyfens and the associated project identifiers are used by the Hyfens
> project. Any trademark rights in those identifiers belong to their
> respective rights holder(s).

Trademark ownership, registration status, jurisdictions, the complete list of
protected identifiers, and the final rights-holder notice remain to be
confirmed. Do not add an `®` symbol or describe a Hyfens identifier as
registered based on this draft.

The neutral attribution above is used until the applicable rights holder and
legal reviewer approve any named rights-holder notice.

## Truthful nominative references

You may refer to Hyfens in plain text when the reference is accurate and does
not imply official status, sponsorship, or endorsement. In particular, these
phrases are permitted when they are truthful:

- **Built with Hyfens** — the product or project actually uses Hyfens.
- **Compatible with Hyfens** — the product or project actually interoperates
  with the identified Hyfens interface, format, or version.
- **Based on Hyfens** — the product or project is in fact based on Hyfens,
  with applicable copyright, license, and notice obligations satisfied.
- **Fork of Hyfens** — the project is an actual fork of Hyfens.

These references should be presented as descriptive statements, not as the
name or logo of an official Hyfens product. Use your own distinctive product
and project branding where confusion is possible.

## Uses that are not permitted

Without permission from the applicable rights holder(s), do not:

- make or imply a false endorsement, affiliation, sponsorship, certification,
  or official identity claim about an independent product, service,
  organization, repository, package, or event;
- use “Hyfens” or a confusingly similar variation as the primary brand for an
  independent product, service, organization, repository, package, or domain
  in a way that suggests official identity;
- misuse, copy, modify, recolor, or use an official Hyfens logo or other
  official brand artwork as an independent brand, avatar, favicon, badge,
  merchandise mark, or promotional endorsement; or
- make a compatibility, affiliation, contribution, or endorsement claim that
  is not true or cannot be supported.

The project’s logos and other official brand assets are not included as a
general branding license. A truthful nominative reference does not authorize
use of an official logo.

## Forks and integrations

Forks and integrations may use their own names and visual identity. A clear
description such as “Fork of Hyfens” or “Based on Hyfens” is acceptable when
accurate. If a reasonable reader could mistake the project for an official
Hyfens project, add a prominent statement that it is independent and is not
affiliated with or endorsed by Hyfens.

Trademark permissions and source-license permissions are separate. Follow the
[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE) and applicable third-party notices for code and
other copyright-protected material, and follow this policy for names and
visual identifiers.

## Review status

This draft is intentionally not a final legal position. Before adoption,
maintainers and legal counsel should confirm the rights holder(s), registration
status and jurisdictions, protected names and logos, permission process, and
any required attribution or disclaimer. Until then, do not represent this
document as a complete trademark clearance or authorization.
